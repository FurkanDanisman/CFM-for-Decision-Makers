What to read from these

verified against the release
01

The distributions are stable in N. The generating SCM does not depend on sample count, so N=200, 500 and 1000 draw from the same law — columns differ only by sampling noise across independent realizations, not by shape.

02

ATE is tighter than CATE, sharing the same center. The green ATE step sits inside the orange CATE fill: averaging over units removes within-dataset heterogeneity but not the realization-to-realization variation in the true effect.

03

Mediated cases spike hard at zero. Backdoor and Frontdoor route the effect only through a hidden mediator (no direct T→Y); many realizations land near a null effect, giving the tall central mass. In the release these two templates are in fact the same graph.

Definitions. CATE is the per-unit effect τᵢ = μ₁(xᵢ) − μ₀(xᵢ), the difference of the structural means under do(T=1) and do(T=0) with the unit's exogenous noise held fixed; each panel pools τᵢ over all 100 realizations. ATE is one number per realization — the mean of τᵢ over that dataset's units — so its histogram has 100 points. A node marked * is unobserved.
