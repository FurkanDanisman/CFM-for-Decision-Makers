Re-centering the treatment effect: β ∈ {−5, −2, +2, +5}

A constant additive effect β·T is injected into the outcome only where T is a direct parent of Y. The four direct-effect case studies re-center on β; Backdoor and Frontdoor route the effect through a hidden mediator (no direct T→Y), so they are left untouched and stay at 0. Shapes and spreads are unchanged — a constant only translates.
