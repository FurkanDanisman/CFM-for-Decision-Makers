\section{Per-Bin Probability \texorpdfstring{$L^2$}{L2} — Density Calculation for Each Method}
\label{app:density-l2}

We compare all methods on a common evaluation grid: $K$ uniform bins on
the scaled response support $[-1, 1]$, with edges
\(
e_0 = -1,\ e_1,\ \ldots,\ e_K = +1
\)
and bin width $w = 2/K$.  For the marginal densities $p(Y_{do(t)}\mid x)$
we report results at $K = 10$; for methods with much finer native heads
(e.g., UWYK with $K_{\text{nat}} = 1000$) we also aggregate to $K = 100$.
For the CATE density $p(\tau\mid x)$ we use $K_\tau$ bins on
$[-2, 2]$ with the same bin width $w_\tau = w$.

For a query $q$ with realised scaling parameters $(y_{\min}, y_{\mathrm{rng}})$
we work throughout in \emph{scaled} $Y$-space: $Y^{\text{sc}} = 2(Y-y_{\min})/y_{\mathrm{rng}} - 1$.
The per-bin $L^2$ distance between method probabilities
$\mathbf{p} = (p_1, \ldots, p_K)$ and truth probabilities
$\mathbf{t} = (t_1, \ldots, t_K)$ is
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:l2-def}
L^2(\mathbf{p}, \mathbf{t}) \;=\; \sqrt{\;\frac{1}{w}\sum_{j=1}^{K} (p_j - t_j)^2\;}.
\end{equation}

\subsection{Ground Truth (Analytic Gaussian per Query)}
For semi-synthetic benchmarks (IHDP, ACIC, polynomial-SCM $d{=}6$) the
per-query outcome distribution is Gaussian in raw $Y$ with mean
$\mu^{\text{raw}}_{t,q}$ and standard deviation $\sigma$.  Transformed to
scaled space, $\mu_{t,q} = 2(\mu^{\text{raw}}_{t,q} - y_{\min})/y_{\mathrm{rng}} - 1$
and $\sigma^{\text{sc}} = 2\sigma / y_{\mathrm{rng}}$.  The truth per-bin
probability is exact:
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:truth-marg}
t^{(t)}_{q,j} \;=\; \Phi\!\left(\frac{e_{j+1} - \mu_{t,q}}{\sigma^{\text{sc}}}\right)
                    - \Phi\!\left(\frac{e_{j} - \mu_{t,q}}{\sigma^{\text{sc}}}\right),
\qquad t \in \{0, 1\},
\end{equation}
where $\Phi$ is the standard normal CDF.  For CATE
$\tau = Y_{do(1)} - Y_{do(0)}$, assuming $Y_{do(0)} \perp Y_{do(1)} \mid X$
(true for IHDP/ACIC/$d{=}6$),
$\mu^\tau_q = \mu_{1,q} - \mu_{0,q}$ and $\sigma^\tau = \sqrt{2}\,\sigma^{\text{sc}}$,
and the truth per $\tau$-bin probability is analogous with
$\{e^\tau_k\}$.  The truth ATE density is the Wasserstein barycentre
of $\{p(\tau\mid x_q)\}_{q=1}^{n_{\text{test}}}$, discretised to the
$K_\tau$ bins.

\subsection{Do-PFN (Adaptive Quantile Bars)}
\label{app:dopfn}
Do-PFN's regression head is a \texttt{FullSupportBarDistribution} with
$K_{\text{nat}} = 100$ \emph{quantile-adaptive} bars.  For each test query the
model returns per-bar logits over borders
$b_0 < b_1 < \cdots < b_{K_{\text{nat}}}$ (raw $Y$ units, learned from
training data — widths span three orders of magnitude, with wide tail
bars and narrow central bars).  We convert to per-bar probabilities via
softmax and rescale borders to scaled $Y$:
\[
\pi_i \;=\; \frac{\exp(\ell_i)}{\sum_{i'} \exp(\ell_{i'})},
\qquad
b^{\text{sc}}_i \;=\; \frac{2(b_i - y_{\min})}{y_{\mathrm{rng}}} - 1.
\]
Under the standard piecewise-uniform density-within-a-bar assumption
implicit in a discrete bar distribution, the exact per-target-bin
probability is
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:dopfn-perbin}
p^{\mathrm{DoPFN}}_{j}
   \;=\;
   \sum_{i=0}^{K_{\text{nat}}-1}
   \pi_i \cdot
   \frac{\mathrm{len}\!\bigl([b^{\text{sc}}_i, b^{\text{sc}}_{i+1}) \cap [e_j, e_{j+1})\bigr)}
        {b^{\text{sc}}_{i+1} - b^{\text{sc}}_i}.
\end{equation}
Because bar edges are not aligned with the uniform $\{e_j\}$ grid,
overlap-fraction weighting is required.  Implementationally,
\Cref{eq:dopfn-perbin} is equivalent to CDF interpolation:
\[
F(y) = \sum_{i:\, b^{\text{sc}}_{i+1} \le y} \pi_i
       + \pi_{i^*}\, \frac{y - b^{\text{sc}}_{i^*}}{b^{\text{sc}}_{i^*+1} - b^{\text{sc}}_{i^*}}
       \quad (\text{$i^*$: bar containing $y$}),
\qquad
p^{\mathrm{DoPFN}}_{j} \;=\; F(e_{j+1}) - F(e_j),
\]
which our code computes via
\texttt{np.interp(edges, borders\_sc, np.concatenate([[0], np.cumsum($\pi$)]))}
followed by \texttt{np.diff}, then renormalised to unit mass to absorb
mass outside $[-1, 1]$.

\subsection{Do-PFN-bb Raw (Native $J{=}10$ 2D Bar Distribution)}
\label{app:bbraw}
The backbone head produces a $J \times J$ joint distribution
$P^{\mathrm{2D}} \in [0,1]^{J\times J}$ on scaled $Y$ with $J = 10$
uniform bins already coincident with the evaluation grid.  Marginals are
therefore direct sums:
\begin{equation}
p^{\mathrm{bb,raw}}_{Y_{do(0)}, j} = \sum_{j'=0}^{J-1} P^{\mathrm{2D}}_{j, j'},
\qquad
p^{\mathrm{bb,raw}}_{Y_{do(1)}, j'} = \sum_{j=0}^{J-1} P^{\mathrm{2D}}_{j, j'}.
\end{equation}
The per-bin CATE probability, assuming independence, is the discrete
convolution of the two raw marginals restricted to the $K_\tau$ diagonal
strips:
\[
p^{\mathrm{bb,raw}}_{\tau, k} \;=\!\!\!\!\!\sum_{(j, j') : e^\tau_k \le (e_{j'} - e_{j}) < e^\tau_{k+1}}\!\!\!\!\!
   p^{\mathrm{bb,raw}}_{Y_{do(0)}, j}\, p^{\mathrm{bb,raw}}_{Y_{do(1)}, j'}.
\]
Under the raw joint (no independence), we instead assign each joint bin
$(j, j')$'s mass directly to the $\tau$ bin containing
$w \cdot (j' - j)$.

\subsection{Do-PFN-bb MALC 1D-marginal ($p_{\text{bb-1D}}$)}
\label{app:bb1d}
Fit a discrete log-concave MLE independently to each raw marginal via
CVXPY (\texttt{malc\_1d\_cvxpy}).  The optimiser returns non-negative
probabilities $\mathbf{q}^{(t)} \in \Delta^{J-1}$ on the same $J = 10$
bins,
\[
\mathbf{q}^{(t)} \;=\; \argmax_{\mathbf{q} \in \mathcal{L}_J}
\sum_{j=1}^{J} p^{\mathrm{bb,raw}}_{Y_{do(t)}, j}\, \log q_j,
\qquad
\mathcal{L}_J \;=\; \{\mathbf{q} \in \Delta^{J-1} : \log q_j\ \text{concave in }j\}.
\]
Since $\mathbf{q}^{(t)}$ lives natively on the $K = 10$ evaluation grid,
$p^{\mathrm{bb,1D}}_{Y_{do(t)}, j} = q^{(t)}_j$ is used directly.

\subsection{Do-PFN-bb MALC 2D-marginal ($p_{\text{bb-2D}}$)}
\label{app:bb2d}
Fit a 2D log-concave mixture $\widehat{h}(y_0, y_1)$ to the joint bar
distribution $P^{\mathrm{2D}}$ (\texttt{fit\_malc\_inner + dmalc\_2d}).
Evaluate $\widehat{h}$ on a fine $n_{\text{eval}} \times n_{\text{eval}}$
grid $\{x_a, y_b\}$ with cell area $\Delta x \Delta y$.  For each target
bin $j$, marginalise and integrate:
\begin{align}
\widehat{h}^{Y_{do(0)}}(x_a) &= \sum_b \widehat{h}(x_a, y_b)\, \Delta y, \\
\label{eq:bb2d-cdf}
F^{\mathrm{bb,2D}}_{Y_{do(0)}}(y) &= \int_{x_0}^{y} \widehat{h}^{Y_{do(0)}}(x)\, dx
   \;\approx\; \frac{\Delta x}{2}\sum_{a: x_a \le y}
       \bigl(\widehat{h}^{Y_{do(0)}}(x_{a-1}) + \widehat{h}^{Y_{do(0)}}(x_a)\bigr), \\
p^{\mathrm{bb,2D}}_{Y_{do(0)}, j} &=
   \bigl[F^{\mathrm{bb,2D}}_{Y_{do(0)}}\bigr]\!(e_{j+1})
   - \bigl[F^{\mathrm{bb,2D}}_{Y_{do(0)}}\bigr]\!(e_{j}),
\end{align}
with the CDF linearly interpolated at $e_j$ and $e_{j+1}$
(equivalent to summing $\widehat{h}$'s per-fine-cell probability over
cells whose midpoints fall in $[e_j, e_{j+1})$, up to trapezoid quadrature
error).  $p^{\mathrm{bb,2D}}_{Y_{do(1)}}$ is analogous.  The 2D CATE
density integrates $\widehat{h}$ over $\tau$-diagonal strips:
\begin{equation}
p^{\mathrm{bb,2D}}_{\tau, k} \;=\; \iint_{e^\tau_k \le y - x < e^\tau_{k+1}}
   \widehat{h}(x, y)\, dx\, dy.
\end{equation}

\subsection{fn=50 (Same as Do-PFN-bb but $J{=}100$ Head)}
Identical recipe to \Cref{app:bb1d,app:bb2d} with $J = 100$.  When the
evaluation grid is $K = 10$, per-bin probabilities are aggregated by
summing every $J/K = 10$ consecutive native bins (widths align exactly
since both grids are uniform on $[-1, 1]$).

\subsection{UWYK-NoAnc and UWYK-FullAnc (Uniform $K_{\text{nat}}$ Bars)}
\label{app:uwyk}
UWYK's monkey-patched \texttt{BarDistribution} head produces per-bar
probabilities $\pi_b$ on $K_{\text{nat}}$ \emph{uniform} bars in scaled
$Y$, with bin width $w_{\text{nat}} = 2/K_{\text{nat}}$.  Bar $b$
represents mass in $[\beta_b, \beta_{b+1})$ where $\beta_b$ are the
uniform borders on $[-1, 1]$.  In our setup $K_{\text{nat}} = 1000$.

Aggregation to any coarser uniform grid $\{e_j\}_{j=0}^{K}$ with $K$
dividing $K_{\text{nat}}$ is exact by \emph{plain summation}:
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:uwyk-sum}
p^{\mathrm{UWYK}}_j \;=\;
   \sum_{b = j\cdot m}^{(j+1)\cdot m - 1} \pi_b,
\qquad
m \;=\; K_{\text{nat}} / K \in \mathbb{N}.
\end{equation}
For $K_{\text{nat}} = 1000$ and $K = 100$: $m = 10$ (every ten
consecutive native probs sum to one target bin).  For $K = 10$:
$m = 100$.  Both cases require no overlap-fraction weighting because
the coarser grid's edges are a subset of the native bar borders.

The independence CATE follows from convolving $\mathbf{p}^{\mathrm{UWYK}}_{Y_{do(0)}}$
and $\mathbf{p}^{\mathrm{UWYK}}_{Y_{do(1)}}$ on the target $K$-grid,
identical to the Do-PFN-bb raw formula in \Cref{app:bbraw}.

The two UWYK variants differ only in the causal-graph adjacency mask
supplied to the shared model — see \citet{uwyk} — not in the discretisation
recipe.

\subsection{Truth and Method ATE via Wasserstein Barycentre}
\label{app:ate}
Given per-query CATE densities $\{p(\tau\mid x_q)\}_{q=1}^{n_{\text{test}}}$
(from any method), we compute the ATE density as their $L^2$-Wasserstein
barycentre on a shared fine $\tau$ grid $\{\tau_c\}$:
\[
p^{\mathrm{ATE}} \;=\; \barycentre_{q=1}^{n_{\text{test}}}
   p(\tau \mid x_q; \{\tau_c\}),
\]
implemented via one-dimensional inverse-CDF averaging
(\texttt{ot\_barycenter.wasserstein\_barycenter\_1d}).  We then apply
\Cref{eq:dopfn-perbin}-style bin aggregation to $p^{\mathrm{ATE}}$ and
$p^{\mathrm{ATE}}_{\text{true}}$ on the same $K_\tau$ bins.  The ATE
$L^2$ uses \Cref{eq:l2-def} with $w \to w_\tau$.

\paragraph{What we do \emph{not} do.}
For any method that outputs a density on a fine grid (e.g., 100 midpoints
on $[-1.5, 1.5]$), we do \emph{not} construct per-target-bin probabilities
by Riemann-summing midpoint values inside each target bin.  Midpoint
Riemann sums do not align with target bin edges when the fine and target
grids are incommensurate, introducing bias of order the fine bin width
per bin edge.  We instead perform per-bin probability integration
directly against the model's discrete probability mass function (or
analytic CDF for Gaussian truth) as spelt out above.
